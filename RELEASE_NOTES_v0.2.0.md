Welcome to **v0.2.0** of **Lucid**! 

This release brings a complete visual redesign inspired by the warm, minimalist LabDrop aesthetic, major accessibility service stability improvements, and fixes for volume button interruptions during mindful pauses.

- 🛡️ **Shielded Mindful Timer**: The 60-second reflection countdown no longer vanishes or restarts when pressing volume buttons, opening quick settings, or receiving system notifications.
- 🔓 **Android 13+ Restricted Settings Support**: Built-in visual guide and direct shortcut to unlock sideloaded accessibility permissions seamlessly.
- ⚙️ **Engine Stability & Zero Zombie States**: Resolved background crash triggers, safe timer interval parsing, and added real-time engine heartbeat tracking.
- 📱 **Universal Device Compatibility**: Unified background run & battery protection instructions across all Android brands.
- ↩️ **Hardware Back Key Handling**: Tapping your phone's Back button on the mindful countdown now cleanly returns you to your home screen.

# 📲 Installation Guide

To ensure a smooth installation, please follow the steps below carefully.

---

## 🔒 STEP 1: Temporarily Disable Google Play Protect

Google Play Protect may flag or block apps installed outside the Google Play Store.

Since Lucid is distributed directly via GitHub Releases, you may need to temporarily disable Play Protect before installation.

### How to Turn Off Play Protect
1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Tap your **Profile** icon in the top right.
3. Select **Play Protect**.
4. Tap the **Settings (⚙️)** gear icon in the top right.
5. Turn **OFF** **Scan apps with Play Protect**.
6. If prompted, tap **Turn Off**.

> **Note:** Please re-enable Play Protect after Lucid has been installed.

---

## 📦 STEP 2: Install Lucid

1. Download **`lucid-release.apk`** from the **Assets** section below.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. Tap **Install** (if prompted, enable *Allow from this source* in your browser/file manager settings).
4. Wait for installation to complete and tap **Open**.

---

## 🛡️ STEP 3: Re-enable Google Play Protect

For your device's security, turn Play Protect back on:
1. Open **Google Play Store** > **Profile** > **Play Protect** > **Settings (⚙️)**.
2. Turn **ON** **Scan apps with Play Protect**.

---

# ⚙️ First-Time Setup (Important for Android 13+)

1. **Android 13+ Restricted Settings** (If Accessibility is greyed out):
   - Go to phone **Settings** > **Apps** > **Lucid**.
   - Tap the **3 dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
   - Tap **Allow restricted settings**, then authenticate with your fingerprint/PIN.
2. **Grant Accessibility**: Open Lucid and tap **Enable** next to Accessibility Service.
3. **Background Protection**: Follow the in-app guide to exempt Lucid from aggressive battery optimization.
4. **Select Target Apps**: Pick the distracting apps you want Lucid to mindfully pause.
